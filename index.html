FAQ


Is there a dress code?

Please take this opportunity to dress up (not costumes!) in suits or equivalent level formal outfits. We request you do not wear white or the green of your invite envelope as these are the colours of the wedding party.


Can I bring a plus one?

Please only bring plus ones if they are named on the invite.


Are children invited?

Please only bring children if they are named on the invite.


Is it an open bar? Can I bring my own drinks?

Please don't bring any drinks (or food) as the venue have strict rules on this. Drinks will be provided for part of the day and the bar will be open to purchase further drinks.


What time do I need to arrive?

Please arrive between 12pm and 12:30pm. The ceremony will start at 1pm. Evening guests please arrive from 6:15pm.
